## Eligibility
- **Student:** HS/college students (2026 graduates eligible), max 5 members + 1 faculty coach, endorsed by school head.
- **Professional:** independent or studio teams, max 5 members.
- One entry per team.

## Key Rules
- **Themes:** Smart City, DRRM, Climate Change, Education, Circular Economy, **Philippine Games and Sports**, Board/Brain Games.
- **Esports-ready:** PvP or PvE with leaderboards; skill/knowledge-based; time-bounded; clear winner per match/round.
- **Platform:** browser, PC, or mobile, with no paid subscription/license needed to play.
- **Publication:** not commercially published; itch.io prototypes are OK if not used in previous competitions.
- **Originality:** all code/logic made by the team. No tutorial/template/open-source code for core mechanics. Personal libraries only if never used in a prior submitted/published game, and must be declared.
- **Mechanics may be derived from existing games** if given a twist (this is how Tilaok! relates to its inspiration). No copied code, art, characters, sounds, or names.
- **AI use:** allowed but must be disclosed (Form 03). Organizers may request prompts, logs, or git history, **so keep this repo's history clean and honest.**
- **Assets:** only team-made, engine-included, or properly licensed.

## Submission Requirements
- [ ] Registration via official link
- [ ] Form 01: Game Development Team Roles
- [ ] Form 02: Waiver and Declaration of Originality (signed; required for judging)
- [ ] Form 03: Asset and AI Usage Disclosure
- [ ] Template 01: Game Title and Synopsis (max 500 words)
- [ ] Game Trailer: 1–2 min .mp4, loopable
- [ ] Prototype/Demo: 3–5 min .mp4 gameplay video (voice or captions)

## Screening Criteria (20% each)
Completeness · Theme Relevance · Originality (incl. Philippine culture) · Gameplay · Aesthetics & Creativity

## Final Judging
| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Gameplay | 20% |
| Esports Potential | 20% |
| Creativity & Innovation | 20% |
| Game Feedback (audience vote from trailer/demo) | 20% |
| Graphics & Art | 10% |
| Music & Sound | 10% |

## Prizes
- **Student (tax-exempt):** ₱50k / ₱25k / ₱15k
- **Professional (taxed):** ₱75k / ₱50k / ₱30k
- DOST covers travel and per diem for 2 representatives per team.

## Strategy Notes
- Only the **top entry per region** auto-qualifies, so the first hurdle is DOST Region VI.
- Audience vote is 20%, so the **trailer matters a lot.**
- Philippine culture boosts Originality, and the Lagsanay/Ilonggo naming helps.
