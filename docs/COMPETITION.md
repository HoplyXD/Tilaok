## Eligibility
- **Student:** HS/college students (2026 graduates eligible), max 5 members + 1 faculty coach (mentorship only), endorsed by the school head.
- **Professional:** independent or studio teams, max 5 members.
- One entry per team.

## Key Rules
- **Themes:** Smart City, DRRM, Climate Change, Education, Circular Economy, **Philippine Games and Sports**, Board/Brain Games.
- **Esports-ready:** PvP (1v1, team vs team, free for all) **or** PvE with leaderboards. Must be skill/knowledge-based, completable in a reasonable time, and have a way to determine the winner at the end of each match or round. _Leaderboards apply to PvE only; Tilaok! is PvP._
- **Platform:** browser, PC, or mobile, with no paid subscription/license needed to play.
- **Publication:** not commercially published; itch.io prototypes are OK if not used in previous competitions.
- **Code originality (§7):** all game logic, mechanics and source code must be created by the registered team members. No code from other competitions, no code copied from other developers, and no open-source/tutorial/template code for core mechanics. Personal libraries only if team-made, never in a prior submitted/published game, and declared. Violations can mean disqualification or prize forfeiture, even after the competition.
- **Mechanics may be derived from existing games** if given a twist. No copied code, art, characters, sounds, or names.
- **AI use (§8), as recorded in the local notes:** allowed for ideation, design, programming, debugging, writing, assets, prototyping, balancing, testing and documentation, but must be **disclosed** (Form 03). Organizers may request prompts, logs, source files or version-control records. Failure to disclose or misrepresenting authorship can mean disqualification. The notes also record §7's registered-team code-originality requirement. These provisions raise an interpretation question for AI-generated code; the obsolete internal interpretation that team members must manually write core code is superseded by the standing workflow in [AI_USAGE.md](AI_USAGE.md). This internal policy change does not rewrite the recorded rules or establish organizer approval, originality acceptance or eligibility. The local notes record AI programming as an allowed disclosed use, but do not conclusively resolve every originality/eligibility question.
- **Assets:** only team-made, engine-included, or properly licensed.
- **Prohibited content:** anything infringing, inappropriate, indecent, obscene, hateful, **torturous**, defamatory, discriminatory or unlawful in the Philippines, including AI-generated content that violates these. For us: soft, comedic deaths only; no gore; no gambling or betting imagery.

## Screening Criteria (20% each)
| Criterion | What they check |
|---|---|
| **Completeness** | Functional, stable, no critical errors or bugs |
| **Theme Relevance** | Core idea clearly reflects the theme; effectively communicates or raises awareness about the theme's goals, values or lessons |
| **Originality** | Logic/mechanics/code by the team; the game has an identity; creative integration of Philippine culture; mechanics derived from existing games with twists; unique/innovative gameplay encouraged; modern tech showcased (physics, networking, procedural generation, AI features) |
| **Gameplay** | Core mechanics and features implemented; **includes instructions, a tutorial or onboarding**; polished and presentation-ready; intuitive, smooth, responsive; basic working UI, easily navigated; progressive loop that is challenging and enjoyable |
| **Aesthetics and Creativity** | Visually appealing art style, effects and colors that match the theme; decent sound design (music, SFX, voice); high-quality graphics and sound |

## Final Judging
| Criterion | Weight | What they check |
|---|---|---|
| Gameplay | 20% | Intuitive, smooth, responsive, easy to learn or master |
| Esports Potential | 20% | Supports competitive play; **balanced mechanics**, no unfair advantage; **rules for winning/losing easy to understand**; **replayable** with different strategies; **exciting and understandable for spectators** |
| Creativity & Innovation | 20% | Innovative, novel or unique mechanics; creative theme capturing different emotions |
| Game Feedback | 20% | Audience vote based on the trailer and gameplay demo shown at the event |
| Graphics & Art | 10% | Appealing art style and effects; **colors accessible and inclusive for all users**; high quality 2D or 3D |
| Music & Sound | 10% | Music and sound complement the environment and show the right emotion |

## Prizes
- **Student (tax-exempt):** ₱50k / ₱25k / ₱15k, plus plaque and medal
- **Professional (taxed):** ₱75k / ₱50k / ₱30k, plus plaque and medal
- DOST covers travel and per diem for 2 representatives per team (student: 1 student + coach; professional: 2 representatives).

## How Tilaok! Fits
- **Strong:** theme (laro ng lahi are the core of the events), Philippine culture, derived-mechanic-with-twist, PvP clear winner, spectator appeal (dynamic camera, interactive crowd), progressive loop (stage grows).
- **Risks and mitigations:**
  - Most rounds are races, not a Filipino game. Show a Lagsanay round in the demo, give the stage a Filipino setting, and keep Filipino-themed items.
  - Theme must be communicated: **"Alam mo ba?" card** before each event.
  - Accessibility: second identifier per chicken besides color.
  - Tutorial is effectively required for screening.
  - Content: soft, comedic deaths; no gambling imagery.
  - Weakest: "modern technologies" bullet; not worth chasing.

## Strategy Notes
- Each DOST Regional Office may endorse up to 3 entries per category; only the **top-ranked entry per region** auto-qualifies as a finalist (the other two are waitlisted). So the first hurdle is DOST Region VI.
- Audience vote is 20%, so the **trailer matters a lot.**
- Philippine culture boosts Originality, and the Lagsanay/Ilonggo naming helps.
