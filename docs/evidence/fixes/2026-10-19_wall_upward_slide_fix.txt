## Cause

`_update_vertical()` only caps positive (downward) locomotion velocity when wall contact is active. The slide cap did not add lift. The actual ascent came from `_try_start_ledge_climb()` accepting an eligible top found by a vertical ray in `find_ledge_target()` without checking that the top collider was the same collider currently contacted by `WallContactProbe`. A separate eligible surface near the chicken therefore looked like a ledge on a tall wall. The climb set locomotion velocity to zero and interpolated the body upward to that false target.

The old climb also assigned `global_position` directly and began its horizontal phase at the same instant its vertical rise finished. That let interpolation cross geometry and made the first diagonal up/over step collide with the wall corner. The updated path validates the vertical and horizontal sweeps at climb start, finishes the rise before the over-edge movement, and checks every interpolated movement against collisions. A blocker appearing during the climb stops that movement step; a fresh attempt remains subject to the same collision checks.
