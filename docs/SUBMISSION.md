## Synopsis outline

Keep the final synopsis below 500 words. It should cover:

1. **Premise:** chickens compete in a wholesome reimagining of sabong.
2. **Core loop:** Pugad → Place → Play, with a stage that persists and grows.
3. **Competitive hook:** Karera races, Lagsanay tag, Kangaroo pressure, scoring, and the first-to-10/12-round match format.
4. **Theme:** Filipino games, language review, Filipino setting, themed items, and educational cards where culturally verified.
5. **Spectator value:** local 2–4 player chaos, readable objectives, crowd response, and event stacking.
6. **Compliance:** original game logic with actual AI assistance accurately disclosed, properly tracked assets, and the §7/§8 interpretation checked against current official instructions.

## Trailer plan

The trailer must read with audio muted because it loops at an event.

| Segment | Visual purpose | Caption requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | Show chickens and the Filipino stage identity. | Title and one-line premise. |
| Pugad | Show visible-item egg choices. | “Pick an egg.” |
| Place | Show simultaneous building and escalating stage clutter. | “Build the course.” |
| Karera | Show flag race and traps. | “Race to the flag.” |
| Event | Show Lagsanay and/or Kangaroo clearly. | State the objective in plain language. |
| Stack | Show one approved Main Challenge + Twist pairing. | Label Main Challenge and Twist. |
| Finish | Show result screen/crowd reaction. | Match goal and title. |

Only capture implemented gameplay. Do not use mockups or concept art as though they are playable footage.

## Demo-video plan

The gameplay video should demonstrate a complete understandable match segment:

1. local join and player identifiers;
2. one Pugad and Place phase;
3. Karera objective and scoring;
4. Lagsanay objective, tagging, and elimination result;
5. one selected item’s tell and counterplay;
6. match or round result and restart flow;
7. a concise explanation of the Filipino-games theme.

Show Kangaroo or an event stack only if they are stable and tested. Captions or narration must explain player decisions, not internal implementation.

## Final build evidence

Before submission, record:

- Godot target version, operating system, target team-PC specifications, controller models, and build identifier;
- supported 2/3/4-player local runs and shared-keyboard limitations;
- full-match and restart results;
- critical known issues and their decision status;
- performance measurement against the agreed target;
- asset credits/licences and AI disclosure confirmation;
- final screenshots/video source files and submission receipt.

## Final review gate

The producer/compliance owner confirms all of the following before upload:

- game is playable and winner determination is clear each round/match;
- tutorial/onboarding is present and accessible;
- build provenance and any AI-generated code are accurately disclosed; no copied code or unlicensed assets are included; the recorded originality requirements and §7/§8 interpretation have been checked against current official instructions;
- no unsupported Filipino-cultural claims remain in public copy;
- no prohibited content, gambling imagery, gore, or uncredited assets remain;
- Form 01–03 and all media are complete and match the final build;
- final submission is made before the official deadline.
